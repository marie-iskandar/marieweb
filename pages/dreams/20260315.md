### events

i was buying an old house to basically start a commune in,
but when we were all trying to decide which room should be whose, we discovered: 
1. the older lady we bought the house from was still living in the attic, 
1. the attic was actually an extradimensional space formed by a contract with a demon, 
1. the lady was actually some kind of evil god and we all basically had, without noticing, passed a challenging test to
	enter or even perceive the attic without having our brains permanently scrambled by the
	evil we had brushed up against, 
1. the attic didn’t have air conditioning or heating so it would have been really uncomfortable anyway.

the rest of the house was also lightly noneuclidean and had a large, but inconsistent number of rooms.
most of them had bizarre or physically impossible properties that made them unsuitable living or work quarters.
this all of course is why a mansion with a pond and garden was only $60k even if it needed some repairs.
for that price we were like, eh. well i guess just let the evil god have the attic, she’s quiet, fun at parties,
and as far as we can tell she just teleports up there sometimes to read romance novels in a giant waterbed undisturbed.
the attic mostly just had three king-sized waterbeds and a bunch of her clothes and stuff.
she dropped snacks off every couple of weeks and hung out for a bit, seemed pretty chill with no obvious evil intentions.

one friend claimed a cozy library that was pretty normal other than being like 6 feet wide by 20 feet long,
but unfortunately we discovered that what we thought was an identical room was actually the same room displaced in time
by like three hours and it ended up being too annoying to figure out how to schedule things for them to share it so
they ended up dividing it in two and just each taking a half. for my own part i mostly ignored the horrors and was
focused on like, minor ceiling repairs.

while the dream consistently stated A Great Evil was afoot, from the perspective of the homeowner,
there was no real sign of that other than doors not consistently leading to the same location,
which was more annoying than anything since if you got lost you’d just wind up in the living room instead of
trapped somewhere.

there were no ghosts (“not real”) or entities or voices whispering forbidden knowledge or anything and it was basically
safe (except for the attic, which we were safe from going forward since we’d already passed its test) so really it seems
like a steal tbh. there *was* a cursed ring, but it didn’t try to tempt anyone to wear it or or misrepresent its nature;
my large communist friend was just like “hell yeah i can finally actually be as cursed as i feel inside” and put it on
and gained the ability to manipulate doorframes to fit furniture through them more easily with no obvious downsides.

there was also a cat sized snail with a big grin that followed people around and wanted to snuggle and eat cake frosting.
we had a priest confirm it wasn’t evil or harmful in any way but he admitted he still wasn't entirely sure what
its whole deal was. cake frosting was a recurring, symbolic motif in this dream, but unfortunately i don't remember why
or what it represented.

### interpretation
i have to confess i haven't read _House of Leaves_ yet, but i'm assuming that it's reasonably similar.
at this point in 2026 our brains have been so relentlessly curbstomped by obligate capitalism that observable cosmic
horror was actually a welcome reprieve from predatory housing prices.

this was not addressed in the dream, but waking marie's interpretation is
that while she did spend the vast majority of her thousands of years of existence as something of an evil god,
at some point she decided she'd rather be a vibrant milf than a cthulhu, and her cultists' religious texts
had simply not been updated yet. many of us can relate to this struggle. i see some people nodding.

and, yes, she was kind of hot. trying to think of a good analogue. maybe kind of a sandra bullock with a pixie cut, if
she were slightly over six feet tall?

yours,
marie, who would love to purchase this house if it were real

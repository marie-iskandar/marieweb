### events

\[redacted because i would love to work there someday\] acquired the rights to "bomberman" and had done a reboot. they said in a pr that they were so excited to be able to "tell stories" in the rich bomberman narrative universe. the bombermen were redesigned to have fruit/vegetable heads, extra-noodly arms and legs, and little katamari-ish faces (no more angry eyebrows or their famous antennae). the white bomberman was an onion, red a tomato, black (now deep-purple) a grape, yellow a lemon, etc. although the game still prioritized explosive shenanigans, the core gameplay was significantly different and attempted to be faster and more vertical: players could jump, run, and crouch/slide. bombs were now thrown instead of placed, either in a line directly outward from the player, in an arc upwards (if crouching). the player could "crouch" once in midair to drop a bomb directly below them, the closet to the "setting" behavior of the original games, which also functioned as a double-jump as they would hop off the bomb they dropped.

the game had a co-op/open world mode with levels adjusted by the number of bombersman currently playing (to indicate when puzzles / etc. required multiple players, platforms / etc. were colored by one of the available players.)

at this point, the dream began to segue into a nightmare with the same basic format.

first, a stern woman's voice and likeness commanded the bombermänner to "FEAST." after picking up enough food, there was an extremely long-unskippable cutscene of the character preparing and then consuming a large meal, with upsettingly realistic/wet sound effects.

the game resumed. the same woman's voice commanded the player to "CREATE." there was a countdown 5, 4, 3, 2, ... 1, 0, that repeated until the player character hit the right input, which caused the player to lay an egg. the egg hatched into a cute little kirby-ish, fox-like creature that followed the player around and performed minor tasks like picking up nearby items. this was apparently a 'squanfy.'

the game resumed and seemed normal for a bit until the same woman's voice commanded, with an even faster and more pressuring countdown, that the player "UPGRADE." the game then vaguely implied that the bomberman and the squanfy borne from their eggs had incestuously mated. the offspring was malformed and horrifying, with rotting skin, exposed bone and sinew, and the general appearance of the [jersey devil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jersey_Devil). it appeared to be the reboot's equivalent of a [louie](https://bomberman.fandom.com/wiki/Louies) and could be ridden as a power-up.

other elements of the dream i can't figure out how to place into the general structure:
* a stand-up comedian repeated the dream above as a pitch to the company not named above
* a man imprisoned in a wheel of cheese and rolled out onstage to replace the scary counting woman from earlier
* a man whose lovers took on his exact apperance, like a sort of STD
* poisoning / antidote shenanigans
* my brain was scanned, revealing that my brainworms were literal slugs and possibly from a potluck guacamole in my youth

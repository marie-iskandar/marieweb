### events

i was vice-hosting a gameshow in which i was an alien/angel (in very intense makeup) named "conchetă"\* delivering judgment on mankind based on various tasks i assigned. contestants were mostly improv comedians. losers had to go to "hell" (a kiddie pool off to the side of the main stage, which was, for whatever reason, a large outdoor tent) and would spend the rest of the episode heckling the remaining competitors. i watched it with some friends, but i didn't remember filming any of it, so watching it was entirely new to me as well.

it was understood that i didn't have the same kind of talent or stage presence as the actual cast, so i while i sometimes spoke, especially to cast judgement, i mostly just sat on a throne or wandered around making alien noises while someone actually competant/funny was my "herald" who spoke on my behalf and called on me when it was time to judge tasks.

the only specific task i remember was "levitate!" -- one contestant solved this problem by painting a stool the color of the background and standing on it. the audience was unimpressed japanese salarymen.

\* apparently, this means "conquest" in romanian, which i do not know?

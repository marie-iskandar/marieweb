### events

i was in toronto. dream toronto had been founded and colonized by finnish pirates. their skeletons were mounted in glass display cases all through the city, and everyone had to work their jobs next to them. if you died on the job, your skeleton also got mounted for your successor to work next to.

there were a lot of very large birds (they looked like turkeys shaved from the neck down, but were like, emu-sized) called "sons of men" who were subject to similar skeletal regulations. places would advertise having them. like you’d go to a store and there’d be signs in the parking lot encouraging you to go see their son of men. they seemed to possess a sort of sad and quiet intelligence; perhaps some knowledge of a previous world.

eventually [zoe kazan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zoe_Kazan) and i decided we needed to leave this place. she drove the getaway car into some hedges and i woke up.
